Block tags (AprilTag tag36h11)

Print at 100% scale ("actual size", no "fit to page"), then check one black square with a ruler. Cut along the dashed lines and glue one tag per face, centered; a block can have tags on any faces (each tag id on one face only, in all your blocks). Matte paper is best -- glare hides tags. Sizes must match the game's settings (Games & apps: Knight on Blocks).
